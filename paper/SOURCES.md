## Rating-rationale registry (§6.1) — public credit-rating disclosures

- Indian rating agencies publish rationales listing rated bank facilities. **CRISIL**
  rationales carry a "Details of Bank Lenders & Facilities" annexure naming individual banks
  + sanctioned amounts; CARE/ICRA/Ind-Ra give facility TYPE + amount only (banks behind a
  separate link). ~40–50% of firms are CRISIL-rated with named-bank annexures. Transcribed
  (via WebFetch, verbatim, with source URLs) in `analysis/pilot_raw/registry_validated.txt`
  (6 large caps) and `registry_midcap.txt` (9 mid-caps: Balrampur, EID Parry, Ashoka, IRB,
  GPIL = PSB by amount; KNR, GR Infra, Shyam Metalics, Sarda = private).
- **Amount-weighted PSB share** = Rs of facilities at PSB banks / Rs at all named banks
  (NBFCs/HFCs excluded). Loaded by `pilot_analysis.load_registry()`; takes precedence over
  AR bank-count where available. Amount-weighting is material: Balrampur is private by
  bank-count but PSB (53%) by rupees.
- This expansion (mid-cap sugar/EPC/steel/fertiliser/paper/agri, PSU-consortium-banked) grew
  the treated group from 7 to **17** (74 firms total, 20 registry-classified firms). PSB by
  amount: Balrampur, EID Parry, Ashoka, IRB, GPIL, Chambal Fert, Deepak Fert, Seshasayee Paper,
  Dilip Buildcon, Gokul Agro.
- **Headline = the FULL 17-treated sample** (not a cherry-picked intermediate), reported honestly:
  quarterly cost-of-debt cushion −0.61pp binary (t=−1.89, 10%), −1.07pp share (t=−1.67, 10%).
  Sign NEGATIVE in every sample (7→17); magnitude/significance NON-monotone: peaked −0.88pp
  t=−2.4 (5%) at 12 firms, diluted to 10% at 17 as heterogeneous PSB borrowers (distressed EPC
  DBL, thin-margin agri Gokul) wash out the average cushion. Capex positive throughout, sig only
  in intermediate samples. This dilution pattern is REPORTED, not hidden — reporting the full
  sample avoids selecting on the outcome. Lesson: pushing N past ~12-15 weakened the result;
  effect is real+modest+sector-heterogeneous, not a clean headline.

## Inference hardening (§6.1, Table: p_t6_inference) — zero new data

- `analysis/inference_tests.py` (deterministic, seed=42, 2,000 draws): exact Fisher
  randomization tests (reassign 17-firm treated set / permute PSB shares across firms;
  Frisch–Waugh residualization on firm+time FE) + placebo shock one year earlier on
  pre-shock data only.
- Results: quarterly cost-of-debt binary p=0.062, share p=0.047; annual capex share
  p=0.035. All placebos wrong-signed and insignificant (+0.53pp se 0.69; +1.27 se 0.90;
  +1.59 se 1.60). Caveat stated in paper: exchangeability approximate (treated firms skew
  high-exposure).
- This addresses the few-treated-clusters objection head-on and is the basis for the
  paper's claim of conventional significance for the amount-weighted treatment.

## Pilot data (§6.1) — fully public, reproducible

The pilot study estimates the design on 54 listed firms using only public data:
- **Firm financials**: [screener.in](https://www.screener.in) company pages, which
  reproduce exchange-filed (NSE/BSE) standalone/consolidated statements. Raw
  transcriptions in `analysis/pilot_raw/data_<TICKER>.txt`; collector script
  `collect_all.py` (fetches + parses the P&L / balance-sheet / quarterly tables).
- **Banker identities**: transcribed verbatim from each firm's annual-report
  "Corporate Information" page; source URL recorded per firm in
  `analysis/pilot_raw/bankers0*.txt`. Banker disclosure is voluntary: 56/80
  targeted firms disclose; 24 are BANKERS_UNKNOWN. 7 of the 54 usable firms are
  majority-PSB.
- **Reproducible**: `PILOT_RAW=analysis/pilot_raw python analysis/pilot_analysis.py`
  regenerates every pilot number and the event-study figure from the raw txt files.
- Honest limitations (all in §6.1): 7-firm treatment group (2 classified on a single
  disclosed banker); voluntary-disclosure selection; sector- not firm-level exposure;
  effective-rate cost of debt with prior-year borrowings at quarterly frequency;
  two-quarter post-window; no ICR<1 firms among large caps (H4 untestable in pilot).
